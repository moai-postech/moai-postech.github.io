Research

Our goal is to design scalable priors for AI. Even in an era of data-centric foundation models, well-designed inductive biases such as domain structure and statistical assumptions improve performance, reliability, and interpretability.

Current directions

Agentic AI

Multimodal LLMs, long-context reasoning, and structured reasoning for agents that decompose a problem and repair their own mistakes.

Physical AI

Vision-language-action models, world models, and adaptation to novel contexts, so that embodied agents generalize beyond their demonstrations.

Manufacturing AI

Time-series analysis and decision-level monitoring for industrial settings, where data is scarce, noisy, and costly to label.

Structural priors for effective learning

Encoding domain-specific structure to improve both accuracy and interpretability.
