Professor

Sangwoo Mo

Assistant Professor, Department of Industrial & Management Engineering, POSTECH
Affiliated with the Graduate Schools of AI, AX, and Industrial Data Science

Before joining POSTECH in 2025, he was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Michigan with Prof. Stella X. Yu, and received his Ph.D. from KAIST under Prof. Jinwoo Shin. He has worked with Google, Meta, and NVIDIA, and received the Samsung Humantech Paper Award and the Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship Korea.
