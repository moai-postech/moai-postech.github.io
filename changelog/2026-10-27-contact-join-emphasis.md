These matter more to us than a long CV." → "**We mainly recruit undergraduate interns**, so that we can grow together over the long term. - 2단계 카드: "Send your CV and transcript / Email them to Prof. Sangwoo Mo." → "Contact us before you apply / Email your CV and transcript to Prof. Sangwoo Mo, for an internship or before applying to graduate school."
