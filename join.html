Join Us

We are looking for people with intellectual curiosity and a genuine passion for AI. A solid foundation in algorithms and coding matters more to us than a long CV.

Topics

  • Multimodal models and agents
  • World models and robotics
  • Industrial applications

How to apply

Send your CV and transcript to sangwoo.mo@postech.ac.kr. Tell us briefly which of the topics above interests you and why.

We mainly recruit undergraduate interns, alongside graduate students through the regular POSTECH admission cycles.

Before you apply

Look through our undergraduate AI study materials to check the background we expect. The study group meets weekly during the semester and is open to POSTECH undergraduates.

Study group contact: Jian Choi (chjohn1105@postech.ac.kr)

The lab also runs a weekly seminar where members present ongoing work. Visitors are welcome.
