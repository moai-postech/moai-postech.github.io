Multimodal Orchestration of Artificial Intelligence

MOAI Lab is a research group at POSTECH building foundation models that orchestrate multiple modalities (vision, language, and physical sensors) into systems that act reliably in the real world.

Focus

Agentic AI

Multimodal LLMs and long-context reasoning: agents that hold a task in mind long enough to finish it.

Physical AI

Vision-language-action models, world models, and contextual adaptation for embodied systems.

Manufacturing AI

Time-series analysis and decision-making for industrial data, in collaboration with domain partners.
