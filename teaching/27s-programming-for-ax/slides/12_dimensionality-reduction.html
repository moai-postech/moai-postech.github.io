PCA With NumPyUse SVD and verify reconstruction directly

X=X-X.mean(0)
U,s,Vt=np.linalg.svd(X,full_matrices=False)
Z=X@Vt[:1].T
print("score shape", Z.shape)
print("MSE", round(np.mean((X-Z@Vt[:1])**2),5))
